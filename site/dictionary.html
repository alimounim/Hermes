Getting recognized

One sign at a timeRaise your hands, make the sign once, then lower them. The top line says "Signing…" while your hands are up.
Lower your hands to finishDrop them below the dotted line on screen (about elbow height) or out of the picture. That is when the sign is judged.
Keep your face and shoulders in viewThe model uses where your hands are relative to your face, so sit back enough to show both.
"Not sure" adds nothingIf it shows "Not sure", just sign again. A wrong word got in? Press U to undo it.
Pause to send3 seconds with no new sign sends the sentence. Press S to send it right away.
What the percentage meansHow often the model added the right word for 4 signers it never trained on. Your own signing may differ: record your versions with record_signs.py to raise it.
Try other versionsVideo pages often show several versions of a sign. If one is not picked up, try another.
