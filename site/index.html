How it works

No app to install. Hermes joins your FaceTime call as the other participant and does the interpreting in between.

  1. You sign

    One sign at a time: raise your hands, sign, lower them. Hermes watches your hands, face and shoulders.

    MediaPipe

  2. It recognizes

    Each whole sign is matched by hand shape, where it is relative to your face, and how it moves.

    Trained on Google's ASL dataset

  3. It speaks

    Pause for three seconds and your signs become one natural English sentence, said out loud.

    Gemini + ElevenLabs

  4. You read the reply

    When the other person talks, their words appear under REPLY on Hermes' video.

    ElevenLabs speech-to-text

Getting recognized

Hermes judges a sign when your hands come down, so give each one a clear start and end.

Show your face and shoulders

Sit back so the camera sees from the top of your head to your chest. Hermes measures where your hands are relative to your face.

One sign, then hands down

Raise your hands, make the sign once, then lower them to your lap or out of view.

Pause to send

Three seconds with no new sign finishes the sentence and Hermes says it.

Not picked up? Sign again

If Hermes isn't sure, it adds nothing rather than guessing. Just repeat the sign.

What to expect

Hermes is a hackathon demo, not a certified interpreter. Here is how it does today.

3 spause, and Hermes says your sentence
  • Small vocabulary. Greetings, needs, feelings, time and family words, chosen for a front desk, pharmacy or clinic. Words outside it are not recognized.
  • One sign at a time. Hermes recognizes separate signs, not continuous signing or fingerspelling.
  • One call at a time. The line is shared during the hackathon. If it's busy, try again in a few minutes.
  • Apple devices only. FaceTime Video works from iPhone, iPad and Mac.
  • Not for emergencies or medical decisions. Use a qualified interpreter for those.

Why "Hermes"?

In the Odyssey, Hermes is the messenger who carries words between gods and mortals. This Hermes carries them between signers and speakers. Built in 36 hours at HackUTA 2026.
